## In one sentence
I picked this video because it shows how to turn boring numbers into an **interactive story** that people can click through, and I wanted to do exactly that with something I really care about: my own Counter-Strike 2 games.

## Why this video and not another one
1. **It fits my interest.** I have played about 210 hours of CS2 (FACEIT and Premier). Every match leaves numbers behind, but FACEIT only shows them as long lists. A story ("you win more on Dust2", "you play worse after a loss") is much more useful to me than a list.
2. **It is something I can actually build.** I don't have a programming background. Streamlit lets you build a website with charts using only Python, with no web design needed. With AI help, I could have a working app online on the first day.
3. **It is about decisions.** The minor is about *data-driven decision making*. A coach that says "ban this map" or "take a break after a loss" helps me make real decisions about how I play.
4. **It is fun.** I want to use the result myself, and my friends I play with can use it too.

## Why the storytelling video is the main one
- **Realtime dashboard:** useful for the layout, but "realtime" is not my problem: my matches only change a few times a day.
- **Movies EDA app:** good for exploring, but it only shows data; my goal is advice and a decision, which is storytelling.
- **CS2 pro analysis:** closest to my topic, but it analyses pros with a fixed dataset; it doesn't show how to build an interactive app. I use it as inspiration for the analysis, not as the main method.
